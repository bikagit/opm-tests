
Results for Pc-Sw and kr-Sw curves are generated with the LS-LB workflow (described in SPE-218427-MS) using a 200^3 cubic subset of a Castlegate sandstone 
pore geometry, publicily available at: Sheppard, Adrian, Prodanovic, Masa. "Network Generation Comparison Forum." Digital Rocks Portal, Digital Rocks Portal, 21 Apr 2025, 
https://www.doi.org/10.17612/P7059V Accessed 9 May 2025.
Porosity: 22.6 %. 
Absolute permeability: 2.47 D.
Interfacial tension: 0.030 N/m.
Contact angle: 20 deg (water-wet conditions).

File "KrPcData_Swi0_06.csv" contains data for primary drainage and subsequent processes with initial water saturation of Swi=0.06 after primary drainage.

Description:
Column 1: "Process" (Process type): 0 = Drainage, 1 = Imbibition
Column 2: "Rev_num" (Reversal number): 0 = Primary drainage, 1 = First imbibition after primary drainage, 2 = Secondary drainage (after first imbibition), 
	   3 = Secondary imbibition (after the secondary drainage from residual saturation after first imbibition), etc. (possibility to add higher numbers based on later simulations).
Column 3: "Sw_rev": Water saturation at reversal point (i.e., the initial water saturation for the current process)
Column 4: "Pc": Capillary pressure [Pa]. 
Column 5: "krw": Wetting-phase relative permeability 
Column 6: "kro": Non-wetting phase relative permeability
